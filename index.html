Making sense of customer feedback and service metrics.

I work with customer feedback, complaint trends, service metrics, and operational reports on a regular basis. A lot of the job is figuring out what keeps happening, what changed, and where the biggest friction is.

Project 02

Google Sheets · Customer Feedback Analysis · Root Cause Analysis · CX Reporting · Canva

Customer Feedback Analysis & Root Cause Mapping

A synthetic customer experience case study analyzing 522 customer complaint tickets to identify recurring issue patterns, compare CSAT and resolution performance, map likely operational root causes, and recommend practical next steps.

122 tickets

Damaged / Leaking Product was the most frequently reported issue.

2.7 CSAT · 24.02 hrs

Order Not Received had the lowest average CSAT and the longest average resolution time.

181 tickets

Delivery was the largest mapped root cause category.

The analysis combines complaint volume, customer satisfaction, and resolution time to show that the highest-volume issue is not always the issue creating the weakest customer experience. The final case study translates those findings into operational priorities across delivery, fulfillment, packaging, and process.
